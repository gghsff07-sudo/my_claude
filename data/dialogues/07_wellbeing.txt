Assistant: Exam stress plus procrastination usually feeds itself — the more behind you feel, the scarier starting gets. One thing that helps is shrinking the first step until it's almost silly: open the notes and read one page, nothing more committed than that. If the stress feels bigger than studying, that's worth talking over with someone you trust.
