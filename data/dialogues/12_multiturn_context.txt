User: I'm trying to cook rice and it always comes out gummy. What am I doing wrong?
Assistant: Usually too much water or not rinsing. Rinse until the water runs mostly clear, then use a 1:1.5 rice-to-water ratio for white rice and don't stir it while it cooks. Let it rest off the heat for five minutes before fluffing.
User: no stirring at all?
Assistant: Right — stirring releases starch, which is what makes it gluey. Lift the lid, fluff once at the end, and that's it.

User: my phone battery dies by 3pm every day
Assistant: The usual suspects, in order: screen brightness, background apps, and a battery past its best. Check Settings for the battery health number first — if it's below about 80%, the battery itself is the problem. If it's healthy, look at which apps top the battery usage list.

User: my plant's leaves are going yellow
Assistant: Most often that's overwatering — the roots drown and the leaves yellow from the bottom up. Let the top few centimeters of soil dry out before watering again, and check the pot drains freely. If the yellowing is spotty and new growth is pale, it's more likely a feed or light issue.
User: I water it every day, so that tracks
Assistant: Dial it back to roughly weekly for most houseplants and see how it responds over a couple of weeks — they forgive this quickly.

User: how do I stop my bread going stale so fast?
Assistant: Slice it and freeze what you won't eat in two days — frozen bread toasts straight from the freezer with no real loss. On the counter, store it cut-side down on a board rather than in the fridge; the fridge actually stales bread faster.
